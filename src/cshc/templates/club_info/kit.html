The club's primary playing strip for home and away games is a purple shirt, navy shorts or skirt, and purple socks. A yellow strip is used for away games when there is a colour clash.

New members must purchase a primary (home) shirt on joining the club. Alternate strip (away) shirts can be provided by the club when needed, but many players prefer to purchase their own, personalized with the same number as their primary shirt.

Colour Clashes

The alternate strip is used away from home against clubs who wear purple or other dark colours, which are indicated by a symbol on the team fixture pages.

Club playing and training kit is available to order from our kit supplier Y1 via their online shop. Most kit is available to order all year round, but some special made-to-order items are only available during set ordering windows.

The playing kit items are all constructed out of high performance fabrics and are selected to complement your club shirt. The training wear is smart and practical and incorporates the club crest and personalised embroidery and printing options.

Some items can be personalised with your name and number. If you don't have a number, please contact the {% clubinfo_email 'KitOfficerEmail' kitofficer_name %} or {% clubinfo_email 'SecretaryEmail' secretary_name %}.
