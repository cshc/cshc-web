Home matches and training are held at Long Road Sixth Form College and St Mary's & Homerton Sports Ground. Both pitches are accessed from Long Road and are easily reached by car, cycle or public transport. Parking is available at both venues.

The nearest train station is Cambridge South, which is approximately a 10-minute walk from Long Road College.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Match Teas" %}

Our post-match teas are usually served at Cantabs RUFC Club House, which is also located off Long Road, a short walk from the college.

When the Cantabs Club House is unavailable, our alternate venue for post-match teas is The Queen Edith, a tradtional pub with a large selection of beer on draught.
