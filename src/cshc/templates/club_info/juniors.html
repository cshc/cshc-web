Juniors

Hockey training for boys and girls aged 6 - 14

{% include 'core/_messages.html' %} {% include 'blocks/_sub_navigation.html' %}

Cambridge South Hockey Club’s junior section trains weekly during the hockey season between September and March. In addition to training, Sunday fixtures are arranged for all age groups against a range of opposition standards to ensure enjoyable and competitive game time for players of every ability in every age group.

We have a dedicated team of qualified coaches, ably supported by parent and player volunteers who coach the Junior section. There is dedicated goalkeeper training for both boys and girls and plenty of club goalkeeping kit to protect them.

As a passionate club, we take a personal approach to promoting the development of our juniors, ensuring plenty of opportunities for everyone to play enjoyable and competitive hockey. We are proud that many of our juniors play for Cambs County, as part of England Hockey's Talent Academies, or at a high level in our senior club.

Read more about South's approach to junior hockey and the practicalities of joining up in the downloadable Junior Handbook in the resources section. We offer a free three-session taster for new players to try out without commitment. The subscription fee is due before your fourth session and if you decide not to continue beyond your first three sessions there is nothing to pay!

No previous hockey experience is necessary and beginners are welcomed. All sticks, balls and equipment are provided, but players may bring their own stick if they have one (please label clearly). Players require comfortable sportswear and trainers with grippy soles, and should bring a water bottle.

Mouthguards and shinguards are strongly recommended for all players.

Junior players who are eligible and also wish to play or train with the club’s senior section (age 13+) are not required to pay separate senior membership subs. Usual senior match fees will be payable.

To apply for a place, or for more information, please fill in the contact form on this page.

Following confirmation of a place, a Registration and Consent form must be completed and returned before a player may attend a session.
