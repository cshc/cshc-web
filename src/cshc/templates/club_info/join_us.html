Cambridge South has men's and ladies' teams across a range of levels from beginner upwards. We welcome anyone interested in playing hockey, whatever your standard or experience.

In line with England Hockey regulations, the minimum age for participation in senior training and matches is 13. Younger players who wish to join the club junior section should use the Juniors Registration form found on the Juniors page

New players who wish to train or play with Cambridge South must complete and return the Player Registration and Consent Form available on this page.

Forms can be returned by email to join@cambridgesouthhockeyclub.co.uk or handed to a club officer or team captain at any match or training session.

You can attend up to three games or training sessions with Cambridge South to try us out before deciding whether to pay your annual subscription and join the club to continue playing.

For players who join part way through the season, a proportional reduction will be made to the annual subscription due. If you join after the New Year then you will usually pay 50% of the normal subscription.

Goalkeepers who own and maintain their own kit pay reduced subscriptions and no match fees!

Current subscriptions rates and information about how to pay can be found under Annual Subscriptions.

Of course, we're sure you'll be champing at the bit to join us and hand over your subscription after your first game!

Goalkeepers who are new to the club have the option of using their own kit or using shared club kit. If using club kit, after playing twelve matches in goal the club will provide a dedicated kit (which remains the property of the club) for the goalkeeper to use.

Goalkeepers who use club kit pay the annual subscription and match fees which are applicable to them. Goalkeepers who provide and maintain their own kit pay the reduced annual subscription, and no match fees.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Umpiring" %} {% clubinfo 'UmpireFee' as umpire_fee %}

Players who umpire receive a payment of £{{ umpire_fee }} per game and for away games can claim a contribution towards travel costs if they take a team player in accordance with the Travel Costs on the relevant venue page.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Travel Costs" %} {% clubinfo 'MileageRatePence' as mileage_rate %}

Players can claim a contribution towards travel costs if they drive to an away game and take at least one other player with them. The contribution is calculated at a rate of {{ mileage_rate }}p per mile based on the round trip distance from Cambridge, rounded to the nearest 5 miles and 50p.

Reimbursement amounts are specificed in the venue information for each away match, and are normal given as credits against match fees.
