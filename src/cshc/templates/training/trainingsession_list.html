Cambridge South runs professionally coached training sessions for men and women of all standards throughout the season from September to March at Long Road Sixth Form College or St Mary's and Homerton Sports Ground. All standard training sessions are free to attend, for club members and newcomers, with no booking required.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Goalkeeper Training" %}

As well as being included within the main men's and ladies' training sessions, the club also arranges fortnightly specialist training for goalkeepers. See the club calendar for the full training schedule. Keepers should arrive early and be kitted up and ready for the start of the session.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="1st and 2nd Team Training" %}

The Men's and Ladies' 1s and 2s run an extra half hour of training on their respective evenings. There is a supplemental fee of £30 per season for this session.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Juniors' Training" %}

For details of Juniors training, including upcoming session dates and times, check out the dedicated Juniors page.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Pay and Play" %}

Cambridge South offers informal "pay and play" hockey at Long Road Sixth Form College almost every week, all year round.

You don't have to be a club member to come along and new players are very welcome. There is the option of higher and lower level small-sided knock-around games, each on a half pitch, or a full pitch all-abilities game on Fridays.

"Lower" games are for players in the 4s and below, and those looking for a more relaxed hockey. "Higher" games are more intense and competitive, for players in the 3s and above.

To play, pre-book for a session via Spond. If you’re new to the club, contact {% clubinfo_email 'SecretaryEmail' sec_name %}, to be registered with us on Spond. The cost is £6 per session or £4 for students and the unwaged, payable on the night via contactless payment.

If you're new to the club, your first session is free!

During the season (September - March)

  • Fridays, 7.30pm to 8.30pm - Mixed half or full pitch all-abilities game

During the off-season (April to August)

  • Mondays, 7.30pm to 8.30pm - Mixed half pitch, split into higher and lower games
  • Wednesdays, 7.30pm to 8.30pm - Mixed half pitch, split into higher and lower games
  • Thursdays, 7.30pm to 8.30pm -
    • Men's higher teams (M1-M3) half-pitch
    • Ladies' half pitch
  • Fridays, 7.30pm to 8.30pm - Mixed half or full pitch all-abilities game
