Pay and Play

Casual hockey for everyone, all year round

Cambridge South offers informal "pay and play" hockey at Long Road Sixth Form College almost every week, all year round.

You don't have to be a club member to come along and new players are very welcome. There is the option of higher and lower level small-sided knock-around games, each on a half pitch, or a full pitch all-abilities game on Fridays.

  • Higher games are aimed at players in the 3s and above, and those looking for a faster, more intense hockey.
  • Lower games are aimed at players in the 4s and below, and those looking for more relaxed hockey.

If you're new to the club, your first session is free!
